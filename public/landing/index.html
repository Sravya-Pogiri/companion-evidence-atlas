01 — The Problem

One attorney. Too many cases. Not enough time.

Public defenders represent people who cannot afford a lawyer. Here is one attorney's working year, hour by hour.

150

150 felony cases per attorney per year: the 1973 national ceiling.1 Historical standard

1973Spread across a 2,080-hour working year, 150 cases leave about 13.9 hours for each one.
