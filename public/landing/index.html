01 — The Problem

The people defending justice need defending, too.

Public defenders dedicate their careers to representing those who cannot afford legal counsel. Yet limited funding, overwhelming caseloads, and inadequate investigative resources leave them fighting an uphill battle.

Behind every case is a person. Behind hundreds of cases is an exhausted attorney, trying to give each person the defense they deserve.

  1. 01Limited government fundingPublic defense offices struggle with insufficient budgets and resources.
  2. 02Staffing shortagesLimited resources make it difficult to recruit and retain attorneys and investigators.
  3. 03Overwhelming caseloadsFewer attorneys must handle more cases simultaneously.
  4. 04Time-consuming investigationsAttorneys spend valuable hours manually reviewing fragmented documents, footage, testimonies, and forensic records.
  5. 05Burnout and exhaustionConstant pressure and emotional strain can contribute to declining morale and attorney turnover.
  6. 06Reduced capacity for thorough defenseFewer resources and less time increase the risk of critical evidence being overlooked.

Stage 4 · Where technology can help

We cannot give public defenders more hours in a day. But we can help them make every hour count.

Meet Lexora.

Lexora brings a case's fragmented evidence into one place, so attorneys spend less of their limited time hunting through it and more of it on their clients.

  • Unifies fragmented evidenceReports, footage, transcripts and filings, side by side.
  • Reconstructs timelinesEvery source placed on one timeline of what happened.
  • Flags potential inconsistenciesWhere accounts may diverge, marked for review, not decided.
  • Source-backed findingsEach finding links to its page or moment so the attorney can check it.

Lexora is a tool for attorneys, not a replacement for them, and it does not fix underfunding. It helps the time they have go further.
